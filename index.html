SHAPE Stats

SHAPE Stats is a private, single-user tool. It reads YouTube Analytics and YouTube channel statistics for the channel owned by its author, so that the author can review the channel's performance. It is not offered to any other user.

Privacy Policy

Data accessed. With the channel owner's OAuth consent, the tool reads read-only YouTube data for that owner's own channel: channel and video statistics, analytics reports, and video metadata. Scopes used: youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly.

Use. The data is used only to produce statistics and reports for the channel owner.

Storage. Data and OAuth tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server operated by this tool.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

Other users. The tool does not access, collect, or store data of any person other than the channel owner.

Revocation. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact. Open an issue in this site's GitHub repository.

隐私政策（中文）

SHAPE Stats 是频道主自用的单用户工具，经频道主本人 OAuth 授权，只读取其本人频道的 YouTube 统计与分析数据，仅用于生成频道主自己的报表。数据和令牌只保存在频道主本人的电脑上，不上传任何服务器，不出售、不与任何第三方共享，不访问、收集或保存频道主以外任何人的数据。授权可随时在 Google 账号权限页撤销。
